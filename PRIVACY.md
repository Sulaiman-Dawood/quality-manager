# Privacy

Quality Manager does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **No data leaves your browser.** The extension has no server, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network requests of its own.
- **Settings stay with your browser.** Your choices (video quality, image size, on/off switches) are saved with the browser's extension storage. If browser sync is on, the browser syncs them like any other extension setting.
- **What it touches on the sites it supports.** On X, Reddit, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok and Bluesky, it reads the video and photo quality lists those sites send to your browser and removes the versions you didn't choose. It also redirects some image requests to a larger or smaller copy of the same image. It doesn't read or change posts, messages or account details.
